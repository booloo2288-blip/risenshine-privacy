Rise'N Shine — Delete Your Data

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open Rise'N Shine on your phone
  2. Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account
  4. Confirm deletion

All your personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to privacy@risenshine.app with the subject "Delete my account" and your registered email address.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your motivational profile (tone, interests, music preferences)
  • Your daily packages archive
  • Your notification settings
