Rise'N Shine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Who we are

Rise'N Shine is a mobile application that delivers personalized morning packages including motivational mottos, weather information, and music recommendations.

2. Data we collect

We collect the following personal data that you provide directly:

  • Full name and email address
  • Communication tone preference
  • Interests and music preferences
  • City (for weather data)
  • Notification time preference

We do NOT collect location data (GPS), social media data without explicit consent, or payment information (handled by Apple/Google).

3. How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to generate your personalized daily morning package, send push notifications at your chosen time, and improve your experience.

4. Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (EU servers — Frankfurt, Germany), compliant with GDPR requirements. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and export your personal data. To delete your account and all data, use the Settings screen in the app. For other requests, contact us at privacy@risenshine.app

6. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • OpenAI — AI-generated content
  • Open-Meteo — weather data
  • Apple/Google — payment processing

7. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
